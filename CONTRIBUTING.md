# Contributing to the public documentation

Thank you for helping make these documents accurate and useful.

## Scope

This repository accepts corrections to its public documentation: broken links, unclear explanations and factual errors. The production application and internal plans are maintained separately.

For a documentation correction, open an issue or propose a pull request with the page, the exact change and a public source that supports it. Please keep the wording clear for an international audience. We may edit, defer or decline suggestions to keep the documentation consistent with the live product and applicable terms.

Do not include credentials, personal data, private conversations, screenshots of other users, unpublished offers or confidential business material. Account and billing support belongs on the [official contact page](https://kom-5a.com/contact). Security reports belong in a [private channel](SECURITY.md).

The [official website](https://kom-5a.com/) and relevant service flow remain the source of truth for live availability, prices and terms.
