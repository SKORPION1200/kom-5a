# A clear product or service listing

People need enough information to decide whether an offer is relevant and whom to contact. The same principles work for a craftsperson, a local business or a digital service.

## Before you publish

1. **Name the offer plainly.** A visitor should understand what you provide from the title alone.
2. **Show the real product or work.** Use images you own or are allowed to publish. Avoid unrelated stock images that could mislead people.
3. **Describe the essentials.** State what is included, location or delivery area, availability and any important limitations.
4. **Keep the price and next step clear.** If the price depends on a quote, say so. Tell people how to reach you through the available platform tools.
5. **Check claims.** Do not promise results you cannot substantiate or use another person's name, logo or testimonial without permission.
6. **Review on mobile.** Make sure the first lines, main image and contact path still make sense on a small screen.

## Simple description template

> **What it is:** [one sentence]  
> **Who it helps:** [the intended customer]  
> **What's included:** [the most important details]  
> **Where and when:** [location, delivery and availability]  
> **Price or quote:** [clear price, range or how to request a quote]  
> **Next step:** [how to contact you]

After publishing, revisit the listing when details change. A well-written listing helps people assess your offer; it does not guarantee views, contacts or sales. Listings remain the publisher's responsibility under the [KOM-5A terms](https://kom-5a.com/terms).
