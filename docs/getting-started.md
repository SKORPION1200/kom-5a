# Getting started with KOM-5A

KOM-5A brings product and service discovery, publishing and community interaction together. You can start on the [web](https://kom-5a.com/) or use the [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/ch/app/kom-5a/id6756894425) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kom5a.mobile) app.

## 1. Start with the public welcome page

Open the [official welcome page](https://kom-5a.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=getting_started) to understand the platform and choose web, iOS or Android. Follow the [product tour](product-tour.md) for a short explanation of publishing, discovery and contact.

When you want to browse publications, choose Explore from the welcome page. A listing or publication can help you learn what is offered and who is behind it. Check the details and the publisher's profile before contacting someone or making a decision.

## 2. Join and participate

Use the [official website](https://kom-5a.com/) to create or access an account. Depending on your account and the current service availability, you may be able to publish, present a product or service, interact with others and use visibility tools.

KOM-5A hosts user-created content. The person or business publishing a listing remains responsible for its accuracy, offer, price, fulfilment and support unless a specific service says otherwise. A listing is not automatically an endorsement by KOM-5A. See [About KOM-5A](https://kom-5a.com/about).

## 3. Choose a visibility option, if useful

The platform includes free-access features and paid professional or visibility services. Each offer has its own eligibility, duration and price or K5A cost. Review the details displayed in the service flow before confirming. Visibility does not guarantee views, enquiries, customers or sales. See the [service terms](https://kom-5a.com/service-terms?lang=en).

## 4. Get help safely

- Account, billing or privacy question: [contact KOM-5A](https://kom-5a.com/contact).
- Possible security vulnerability: [private reporting instructions](../SECURITY.md).
- Error in this public documentation: [suggest a correction](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Never post passwords, payment information, private messages or another person's data in a public GitHub issue.
